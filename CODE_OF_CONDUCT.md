# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community.
Contributors must communicate constructively, accept technical feedback, respect
privacy, and avoid sharing confidential operational data. Unacceptable behavior
includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Maintainers may
remove content, restrict participation, or apply other proportionate corrective
actions. This policy is based on the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
